LAND & ADVENTURE

Mountains, trekking & hiking

Explore rugged highland landscapes, scenic trails and nature study opportunities. Mt Wilhelm is one of the country's best-known mountain destinations.

CULTURE & COMMUNITY

Traditional cultures & arts

Learn about local customs, arts, traditional bamboo flute music and fire-making practices through respectful, community-led experiences.

WILDLIFE & WATER

Nature, birds & diving

Bird watching, including the Bird of Paradise, tropical nature study and Coral Sea diving can bring you closer to PNG's remarkable biodiversity.

CAVES & CLIMBING

Cave exploration & rock climbing

Ask about suitable cave and climbing outings, local conditions, safety requirements and the right level of experience for your group.

FESTIVALS

Highland cultural shows

Ask about the Mt Hagen Cultural Show, usually held in August, the Goroka Cultural Show, usually held in September, and regional festivals such as Simbai. Confirm dates each year.

DISCOVER THE COUNTRY

A thousand reasons
to explore.

Papua New Guinea brings together highland peaks, tropical coastlines, rainforest, wildlife and diverse living cultures.

HIGHLANDS

Mountain country.

The highlands offer dramatic mountain scenery, trekking opportunities and cultural experiences. Mt Wilhelm is a major draw for hikers, while Mt Hagen and Goroka are known for their cultural shows.

  • Mt Wilhelm — mountain trekking
  • Mt Hagen — cultural show season, commonly August
  • Goroka — cultural show season, commonly September
  • Simbai — ask about festival dates and access
COAST & ISLANDS

Blue water. Green worlds.

PNG's coastal and island environments are known for tropical scenery and marine biodiversity. Ask about Coral Sea diving, nature outings and travel logistics that suit your itinerary.

  • Coral Sea diving possibilities
  • Tropical coastlines and island scenery
  • Rainforest and wildlife observation
  • Bird watching and nature study
TRAVEL WELL

Plan with local context.

Road access, weather, permits, accommodation and festival dates can vary. Confirm details before travel and follow guidance from your local guide.

CULTURE WITH RESPECT

Travel with curiosity. Leave with understanding.

Papua New Guinea is home to hundreds of distinct language and cultural communities. Traditions are living, local and diverse; no single experience can represent the whole country. The best visits begin by listening to hosts, asking permission and respecting local protocols.

  • Follow the guidance of local hosts and guides.
  • Ask before photographing people or ceremonies.
  • Respect sacred places, community rules and local customs.
  • Support local businesses and community-led experiences.
